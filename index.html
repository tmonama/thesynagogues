Welcome

Raising a generation of purpose-driven believers who carry the Kingdom of God.

The Synagogues JWC was officially launched on 5 June 2010 in Pretoria by Apostle Glen Monama. What began with his wife and four children in a home has grown into a multi-campus ministry spanning Vereeniging, Sonlandpark and Pretoria East.

Our calling is to teach an undiluted and uncompromised Word, without fear or favour — and to raise a people who walk in purpose. Whether you are seeking God for the first time or going deeper, you are welcome here.
