The Vision

God is still speaking.
And what He speaks, He reveals.

Not a crusade. Not a convention. Six nights set apart for the prophetic.

There are answers that have been hidden, doors that have stayed closed, and seasons that have not made sense. Many have prayed and waited without knowing which way to turn.

For six nights, the voice of God will go forth through His servants. What was concealed will be uncovered, and lives will receive clear direction from Heaven.

“Surely the Lord God will do nothing, but he revealeth his secret unto his servants the prophets.”

Amos 3:7

What to Expect

Come with a question.
Leave with a word.

I
The Prophetic Word

Accurate, specific words from the mouth of the Lord, to individuals, families and the house.

II
Hidden Things Revealed

What has been concealed in the spirit brought into the light.

III
Direction

Clarity for decisions, careers, ministries, businesses and relationships.

IV
Alignment

Destinies realigned to God’s timing; lost years restored by His word.

V
Prophetic Teaching

How to hear God for yourself, and how to receive and walk out a word.
