What to expect

Arrive a little early

Doors open 1 hr before each service. Arriving early gives you time to find a seat, meet a host, and settle in before things start.

Come as you are

Smart casual is the norm. We value your presence over your outfit. There is no dress code — come as you are and be received as family.

A host will welcome you

First-time visitors receive a personal welcome from one of our hosts, who will help you find your seat, answer questions, and connect you to the family.
